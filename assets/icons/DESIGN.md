# Move Strong icon

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, then exported at 192 × 192 and 512 × 512 for the app manifest. The transparent first-pass mark supplies the 96 × 96 Android notification badge. Previous icon files remain available but are no longer referenced by the app.

Design prompt: A finished square Android app icon for Move Strong, with a bold pale lime abstract M formed from rising athletic strokes, suggesting forward motion and strength. Centred, rounded geometric mark within the central 60% of the canvas, on a full-bleed deep forest green background. No text, border, phone mockup or exterior rounded corners.

Final edit prompt: Preserve the centred lime mark and replace the transparent background with opaque deep forest green #20352d extending to all corners. Keep the mark safely inside the Android circular crop.

Files: `move-strong-192.png`, `move-strong-512.png`, `badge-96.png`. Versioned filenames and service-worker cache avoid retaining the old in-app asset. Installed Android launcher icons can update later than the webpage.
